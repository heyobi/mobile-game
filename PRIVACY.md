# Neon Swarm — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 14 November 2026_

Neon Swarm ("the game") does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal data.

- **No account, no sign-in.** The game can be played without providing any information.
- **Data on your device only.** Your progress (crystals, upgrades, best times) is saved locally on your device.
  It is not sent to us or to any third party, and it is deleted when you delete the game.
- **No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs** are included in the current version.
- **TestFlight:** if you test a beta through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and feedback you choose
  to send with the developer, under [Apple's TestFlight terms](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/testflight/sren/terms.html).

If a future version adds features that process data (for example leaderboards or optional ads), this policy will be
updated before that version is released.

Contact: open an issue at <https://github.com/heyobi/mobile-game/issues>.
